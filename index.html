Concept project · Juniper Lane

Seasonal concept board

Juniper Lane is a fictional plus-size fashion brand I created to show the kind of work I do in-house. Before a season moves into production, leadership needs one view of the story: mood, color, silhouettes, and what's been decided.

I built the board the way I run reviews in Miro: a clear left-to-right flow, color with usage ratios and lab-dip notes, and a last frame that records decisions and next steps so nothing gets lost after the meeting.

Made
Concept board, color story, silhouette sketches, review log
Tools
Miro, Illustrator, Photoshop
Concept project · Juniper Lane

Line review deck & template

The concept board becomes a leadership deck. I built a PowerPoint template with locked layouts and theme colors, so merch and design teams can update it without breaking the brand.

Every slide title states the takeaway ("Dresses carry the season at 38% of the line"), and the deck ends with the specific decisions leadership needs to make.

Made
8-slide .pptx, cover and content layouts, native editable charts

Concept project · Juniper Lane

Line sheet & in-store print spec

Once leadership approves the line, two documents carry it forward. The line sheet puts all 8 styles on one page for sales and buyers: style numbers, colorways, size range, fabric, and pricing.

The print spec is what reaches the printer for the in-store kit: trim, bleed and safe areas, stock, quantities for 142 stores, kitting, and a schedule that works back from the in-store date.

Made
Line sheet PDF, print spec PDF
Tools
InDesign, Illustrator

About

I'm a visual and production designer with more than 10 years supporting an in-house retail brand. I work between designers, art directors, product teams, and vendors, making sure what gets approved is what gets produced.

The projects here use a fictional brand, so I can share them publicly without showing my employer's confidential work. Each one recreates a type of work I do every day. I use generative AI tools to explore concepts and speed up production; final artwork and brand decisions are always mine.
